    FAQ

    Common questions.

    Is Setout free?
    You get full access free for 2 weeks. After that it's just $1.99/week, and you can cancel anytime.
    Does it work without internet?
    Yes, for the calculators. All calculations run on your device, so they work on remote sites and underground. You only need a connection to download the app and start or manage your subscription.
    Which standards does it follow?
    Setout is built for Australian and New Zealand construction standards. Compliance notes are built into each calculator where relevant.
    Can I rely on the results?
    Setout is a calculation aid. Results are a guide only and don't replace the National Construction Code, the NZ Building Code, manufacturer specs or an engineer's advice. Always check against current requirements before you build.
    Can I save my calculations?
    Yes. Save any result to a named job. Your history is stored locally on your phone — no account or cloud sync required.
    Is there an Android version?
    Setout is launching on iPhone first, with Android to follow. Join the launch list and we'll email you when each version is available.
