1

Answer, first thing.

A sticky banner pins the headline result to the top of the scroll — the number you'd read out at the yard counter. It never scrolls out of sight while you check the details underneath.

2

Inputs step aside.

Once you've calculated, inputs collapse into a one-line summary. Tap to expand, edit any field, the bar below flips to "Results out of date" — one tap to refresh.

3

Everything else, on demand.

Working steps, secondary counts, waste factors and compliance stay a tap away rather than filling the screen — the tradie sees them when they want them, not when they don't.

4

Recalculate is always reachable.

A sticky bottom bar shows the state at a glance — green when current, amber when a value has changed. No hunting for the Calculate button when you're testing a different board width.
