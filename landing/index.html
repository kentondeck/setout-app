Built out of
frustration.

Generic calculator apps don't understand how building works. Wrong units, missing outputs, no compliance context. We built Setout because we kept running into the same gaps on site.

Every calculator in the app comes from a real problem. If it's in there, it's because someone needed it and nothing else did it properly.

We're based in Australia and building specifically for the AU and NZ construction industry. More calculators are in the pipeline.
