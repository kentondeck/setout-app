Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Built by tradies, for tradies. Every feature exists because someone on site needed it.

Voice Input

Speak your measurements hands-free. Setout converts your voice to numbers instantly — no typing required.

Apprentice Mode

Every calculation shows its full working step by step. Perfect for training or double-checking your numbers.

AU & NZ Standards

Compliance notes built into every calculator. Stair limits, stud spacing, wind zones — all referenced automatically.

Job Sheets

Save any calculation to a named job. Pull it up on site, share with your team, or keep it for your records.

Works Without Signal

No internet required. Everything runs on your phone — remote sites, underground, anywhere.

Diagram Output

Key calculators generate visual diagrams — stair stringers, roof pitches, spacing layouts — that you can save and share.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is Setout really free?
Yes — completely free to download and use. No subscription, no in-app purchases, no account required.
Does it work without internet?
Completely. All calculations run on your device. No data connection needed — works on remote sites, underground, anywhere.
Which standards does it follow?
Setout is built for Australian and New Zealand construction standards. Compliance notes are built into each calculator where relevant.
Can I save my calculations?
Yes. Save any result to a named job. Your history is stored locally on your phone — no account or cloud sync required.
Is there an Android version?
Both iOS and Android versions are coming soon. Sign up to be notified when it launches.
