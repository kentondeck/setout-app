Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This policy explains what information Setout collects and how we handle it, both in the Setout app and on this website (setoutapp.co.nz). We built Setout to work on your phone without an account, so we collect very little.

Who we are

Setout is operated by Setout Holdings Limited. You can contact us at info@setoutapp.co.nz.

The app

You don't need an account to use Setout. Your calculations, saved jobs, receipts and tool log are stored on your own device. We do not receive or store this content on our servers.

Camera, photos and files. Setout may ask for access to your camera, photo library or files when you use a feature that needs it. Anything you add stays on your device, and it is only shared if you choose to share it, for example by sending a PDF to your team.

Analytics and crash reports. The web version of Setout uses Umami, a privacy-focused analytics service, to count visits. The iPhone app does not use analytics. We may use Sentry to receive crash and error reports from the app; these reports do not include your name or email and are used only to fix bugs. Setout does not use advertising trackers.

Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play, and we never see or store your card details. We use RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) to manage subscriptions and unlock features. RevenueCat receives purchase information from the store, such as your subscription status and an anonymous identifier, and handles it under its own privacy policy. RevenueCat is a US-based service, so this information may be processed in the United States.

This website

Our website is hosted by Vercel, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and performance. The site loads the Inter font from Google Fonts, which means your IP address is sent to Google when the page loads.

Sharing your information

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers named above, where needed to run the website, or where the law requires it.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you by writing to info@setoutapp.co.nz. We handle requests in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Australia) and the Privacy Act 2020 (New Zealand). If you're unhappy with how we respond, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner or the NZ Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

Children

Setout is a tool for people working in construction and is not aimed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions? Email info@setoutapp.co.nz.
