Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2025

The short version

Setout stores everything on your device. We don't collect your data, don't track you, and don't sell anything to anyone. The app works fully offline and no information about you or your work ever leaves your phone.

What we collect

Setout collects minimal information to make the app work:

  • Your first name — entered during onboarding, used only to personalise the greeting on the home screen.
  • Your region (Australia or New Zealand) — used to show the correct building code references and tax terminology.
  • Your calculator results and jobs — saved locally so you can access your history and saved jobs.
  • Your app settings — preferences like unit system (metric/imperial) and pinned calculators.

All of this information is stored only on your device using standard iOS local storage. None of it is ever transmitted to us or any third party. The app works fully offline.

What we don't collect

  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not collect your contacts, photos, or any other device data.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not serve ads.
  • We do not create accounts or require sign-in.
  • We do not share any data with third parties.

Quotes and PDFs

When you generate a quote or invoice PDF, the document is created entirely on your device. If you choose to share it, that is done through iOS's standard share sheet — Setout does not transmit the PDF anywhere.

Children

Setout is designed for use by construction professionals and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
